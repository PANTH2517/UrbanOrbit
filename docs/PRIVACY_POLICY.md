# UrbanOrbit — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: [fill in launch date]. Replace bracketed placeholders before
publishing.*

UrbanOrbit ("we", "the app") lets citizens report urban issues and lets
verified government officials triage and resolve them. This policy explains
what we collect, why, and who can see it.

## What we collect

| Data | From whom | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Full name, email | Citizens & officials, at signup | Account identification |
| Phone number (SMS-verified) | Citizens | Anti-spam / accountability for reports; see `docs/SECURITY.md` §3 |
| Department, employee ID, authorization document | Officials, when applying | Manual verification of official status by an admin |
| Report content: title, description, photo, location (lat/long, address) | Citizens, when filing a report | The core civic-reporting function; **published publicly** as part of the transparency map |
| Status/priority updates, assigned official | Government officials | Case management |
| Action logs (who approved/rejected an official, who changed a report's status) | Generated by the system | Accountability/audit trail (`docs/SECURITY.md` §5) |

We do not collect payment information, government ID numbers (e.g. Aadhaar),
or biometric data. See `docs/SECURITY.md` §3 for why full Aadhaar/eKYC
verification is explicitly not implemented.

## What's public vs. private

- **Public**: submitted issue reports (title, description, photo, location,
  status) — this is by design a public civic-transparency map. Do not include
  personal information in a report's description.
- **Private**: your email, phone number, password (never stored by us in
  plain text - Firebase Authentication manages it), and — for officials — your
  employee ID and authorization document, visible only to you and admins.

## Where it's stored

All data is stored in this project's Firebase (Google Cloud) project. Access
is restricted by Firestore/Storage Security Rules as described in
`docs/SECURITY.md`.

## Data retention

- Issue reports are retained indefinitely as part of the public civic record,
  unless removed by an admin (e.g. abuse, duplicate, or a citizen's request).
- Rejected/revoked official applications and their uploaded documents are
  retained for [fill in retention period, e.g. 90 days] for audit purposes,
  then deleted.
- You may request deletion of your account and associated non-public data by
  contacting [fill in contact email].

## Third parties

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Functions,
  SMS delivery for phone verification).
- **Google Gemini API** — issue title/description/location are sent to
  generate an AI triage recommendation, only when a verified official
  requests it from the dashboard. No citizen personal data (name, email,
  phone) is included in that request.

## Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data
by contacting [fill in contact email]. Public issue reports may require admin
review before removal, since removing a report also removes it from the
public record other citizens rely on.
