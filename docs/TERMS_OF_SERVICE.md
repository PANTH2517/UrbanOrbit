## 1. What UrbanOrbit is

UrbanOrbit is a civic-issue reporting platform. Citizens report urban
problems (potholes, waste, unsafe areas, etc.) with a location and optional
photo; verified government officials triage and track resolution.
UrbanOrbit is **not** an emergency service — for emergencies, contact local
emergency services directly, not this app.

## 2. Accounts

- Citizens create an account with an email, password, and a verified phone
  number (see `docs/PRIVACY_POLICY.md`).
- Government officials create an account and submit a verification
  application; dashboard access is granted only after admin approval
  (`docs/SECURITY.md` §4). Submitting false credentials or an authorization
  document you are not entitled to use may result in account termination and,
  where applicable, referral to the relevant authorities.

## 3. Acceptable use

You agree not to:

- Submit false, defamatory, or misleading reports.
- Upload photos that violate others' privacy or contain illegal content.
- Attempt to access another user's account, or the government dashboard,
  without authorization.
- Use automated means to scrape, spam, or overload the service.

We may remove content or suspend accounts that violate these terms.

## 4. No guaranteed response time

UrbanOrbit is a reporting and tracking tool. Submitting a report does not
guarantee any particular response time, outcome, or that a government body
will act on it. Response times depend entirely on the relevant government
department's own processes and resources.

## 5. Public data

Issue reports (title, description, photo, location, status) are public by
design, to support civic transparency. Do not include personal or sensitive
information in a report.
